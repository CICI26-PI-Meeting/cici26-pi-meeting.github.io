The NSF is committed to promoting safe, productive research and education environments for current and future scientists and engineers, including at the PI meetings. It is NSF policy to foster harassment-free environments wherever science is conducted, including at NSF-sponsored meetings. NSF will not tolerate sexual harassment, other forms of harassment, or sexual assault.

The NSF [Stopping Harassment and Assault](https://new.nsf.gov/stopping-harassment) provides additional information regarding NSF's approach to combating harassment and sexual assault, prohibited behaviors and definitions, and NSF's Office of Equity and Civil Rights.

For reporting violations of the code-of-conduct during or after the CICI PI meeting, please talk to any of the [coordinators](/#coordinators) or email [cici2026pimeeting@gmail.com](mailto:cici2026pimeeting@gmail.com). Any complaints received will be thoroughly investigated. We are committed to protecting the confidentiality of your communication.

This code-of-conduct will also be disseminated to the PI meeting participants.
