The CICI PI Meeting will be hosted at the **UCLA Meyer and Renee Luskin Conference Center**, located on the UCLA campus.

### Check-In

A registration desk will be set up near the entrance of the meeting space. Check-in will open on the morning of Day 1 (Sunday, May 11). Please plan to arrive no later than 8:30am.

### On-Site Amenities

The Luskin Conference Center offers a variety of on-site amenities, including:
- [Plateia restaurant](https://luskinconferencecenter.ucla.edu/dine/) for dining outside of meeting-provided meals
- Business center and lounge areas
- Hotel accommodations for those staying on-site ([book at luskinconferencecenter.ucla.edu](https://luskinconferencecenter.ucla.edu/))
