The CICI PI Meeting will be hosted at the UCLA Meyer and Renee Luskin Conference Center.

Parking is available in the nearby Parking Structure 8 garage on the roof for $17 per day (overnight parking is available). You must enter the parking garage through the [Structure 8 Driveway from Westwood Plaza (Google Maps link)](https://maps.app.goo.gl/tv1ZvT5tA9ZgM2gq9), park in a designated visitor parking spot, and pay using the pay station.

Many public transit busses also stop in front of the venue. Payment for these busses can be done with cash or by creating a digital TAP card using Apple Wallet or the TAP card app.
