The CICI PI Meeting is organized to support collaboration and knowledge sharing
among Principal Investigators funded by the National Science Foundation's
Cybersecurity Innovation for Cyberinfrastructure (CICI) program.

Participants are invited to exchange project outcomes, discuss common
challenges, and explore new opportunities for collaboration in advancing secure
and resilient cyberinfrastructure.

For current logistics and updates, please visit the Registration, Venue, and
Schedule pages in the site navigation.
